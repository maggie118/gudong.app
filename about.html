About Gudong
一个发现中国古董的地方 A Place to Discover Chinese Antiques
古董集是新加坡本地的古董与收藏品信息发现平台，帮助收藏者发现值得了解的中国古董。 Gudong is a Singapore-based discovery platform for Chinese antiques and collectibles.

我们的出发点

Why We Started

中国古董的买卖一直分散在画廊、拍卖行与私人间。信息零散、门槛不低，许多收藏者想了解一件器物，却不知道从何看起。

Trading Chinese antiques has long been scattered across galleries, auction houses and private hands. Information is fragmented, and the barrier to entry is high — many collectors want to understand a piece but don't know where to look.

Gudong 想做一件简单的事：把藏品信息整理清楚，让更多人能看见、能了解，并能直接联系卖家。

Gudong does one simple thing: organize listing information clearly, so more people can see it, understand it, and reach the seller directly.

我们不做什么

What We Don't Do

为了让边界清晰，有些事 Gudong 不做：

To keep the boundary clear, here's what Gudong does not do:

  • 不鉴定真伪，不提供权威鉴定报告No authentication or authoritative appraisal reports
  • 不估价、不参与定价No valuation or pricing involvement
  • 不代收款、不代付款、不托管资金No payment processing or fund escrow
  • 不参与买卖交易，不向卖家抽取成交佣金（委托找货为买家自愿购买的独立服务，收费见价格页）No transaction participation and no sales commission charged to sellers (the Sourcing Service is an optional, independent buyer service — see Pricing)
  • 不担保货品真伪与成交结果No guarantee of authenticity or completion
