为何建立古董圈

Our Founding Philosophy

数百年来，中国古董的品鉴与流通始终依托高门槛的私人圈层与传统拍卖行。优质藏品的信息却往往零散而隐秘——买家难觅心仪之物，卖家亦难跨越地域，寻得真正懂货的"对眼人"。

For centuries, the connoisseurship of Chinese antiques has relied on private networks and traditional auction houses. Yet the finest pieces remain scattered and hard to find — leaving discerning buyers without access, and seasoned sellers far from collectors who truly understand their objects.

古董圈 应运而生。 我们不做传统意义上的交易中介，而是以现代数字技术，将散落于全球画廊与藏家手中的珍罕器物，以高标准、结构化的方式呈于台前，让每一件古物都能讲出它背后的历史与审美。

Gudong.app was created to bridge this gap. We are not a traditional intermediary. Instead, we use modern digital standards to curate and present fine objects from global galleries and private collections, letting each piece speak for the history and beauty it carries.

行规契约与中立声明

Our Commitment to Neutrality & Protocol

古董收藏的核心魅力，在于眼力的交锋与知识的积累。为了维护健康、纯粹的交流环境，古董圈 恪守行业规矩与中立立场：

The core charm of antique collecting lies in connoisseurship and knowledge exchange. To maintain a healthy and authentic environment, Gudong.app strictly adheres to industry protocols and absolute platform neutrality:

我们坚持做纯粹的技术服务者与信息撮合者，并恪守以下边界：
  • 尊重眼力，不作伪权威： 平台不提供官方鉴定报告，不强制为藏品背书。古物真伪与品相均遵循行业“买者自负”原则，由买卖双方自行研判与考证。 Connoisseurship First: We do not issue official authentication reports or enforce platform guarantees. Authenticity and condition are evaluated directly between buyers and sellers following traditional market practices.
  • 中立独立，不参与交易抽佣： 平台不干涉藏品定价，不收取任何交易抽佣，亦不托管买卖双方资金，彻底杜绝利益冲突。 Zero Transaction Commission: We do not take commissions on sale prices, participate in negotiations, or escrow funds. Our neutrality ensures unbiased object exposure.
  • 保护私密，无打扰沟通： 买卖沟通由双方直接进行，平台不截留客户资源，亦不向第三方披露藏家的私密收藏隐私。 Collector Privacy: Client interactions remain strictly direct and confidential, preserving privacy for high-net-worth collectors.

A natural gateway between Eastern and Western art markets, Singapore offers free-port logistics, stringent privacy laws and a robust legal system. On this foundation we connect global Chinese communities with international collectors — a trusted, refined place to discover objects.
