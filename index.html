常见问题Frequently Asked
01古董圈是什么？What is Gudong?
古董圈是古董信息发现与藏家连接平台。买家可免费浏览、收藏藏品并直接联系卖家。
Gudong is a platform for discovering antiques and connecting collectors. Buyers can browse and save items, and contact sellers directly for free.
02怎么购买藏品？How do I buy an item?
在藏品详情页查看卖家联系方式，直接与卖家沟通，确认藏品情况、价格及交付安排。买卖双方直接交易，平台不经手付款。
Use the contact details on the item page to speak with the seller and confirm the item's condition, price, and delivery arrangements. Buyers and sellers transact directly; Gudong does not handle payment.
03藏品价格是谁定的？Who sets the item prices?
藏品价格由卖家自行设定，平台不定价。标注“私聊询价”的藏品，请直接联系卖家了解报价。
Sellers set their own prices; Gudong does not price items. For items marked “Enquire”, contact the seller directly for a quote.
04古董圈提供鉴定吗？Does Gudong authenticate antiques?
不提供官方担保。古董圈作为去中心化网络不参与交易，但为了给全球藏家提供决策参考，我们支持引入独立的第三方付费顾问意见。买家可在藏品页一键向平台顾问网络发起私密学术咨询。
We provide no official guarantee. As a decentralised network, Gudong does not take part in transactions; to support collectors' decisions worldwide, we facilitate access to independent third-party paid advisory opinions. Buyers can initiate a private academic consultation with the platform's advisor network in one click from any item page.
05如何成为藏家 / 发布藏品？How do I become a collector or list an item?
点击“了解藏家服务”，查看入驻说明并按页面指引联系平台。成为藏家后，可提交藏品资料与图片进行发布。
Select “Learn about Collector Services” for joining information and follow the page instructions to contact the platform. Once onboarded, submit item details and photos for publication.
06古董圈会抽取佣金或经手付款吗？Does Gudong charge commission or handle payment?
完全不会。平台收取 0% 佣金，也不经手任何资金、不做资金托管。买家与卖家通过 WhatsApp 私密直连后自行议价、自行结算；古董圈只提供信息展示与发现，不参与成交环节，也不从中抽成。
Never. Gudong charges 0% commission and never handles or holds funds. Buyers and sellers negotiate and settle directly over a private WhatsApp line. The platform only provides listing discovery — it takes no part in the transaction and takes no cut.
