常见问题Frequently Asked
平台会帮忙鉴定藏品吗？Does Gudong authenticate items?

No. Gudong is a discovery platform and does not provide authentication, valuation, or guarantees. All descriptions and images are seller-provided — verify independently or consult a specialist.

Yes. On request, the platform can refer you to a specialist for a personal opinion, for reference only. This does not constitute authentication. Whether to accept it is your call; the platform does not intervene or guarantee. Fees and conclusions are handled directly between you and the expert.

Contact details are shown on the item detail page. Buyers and sellers communicate directly; the platform does not handle transactions.

不是。所有价格由卖家自行设定，平台仅提供展示。部分藏品为「私聊询价」，需直接联系卖家获取报价。
