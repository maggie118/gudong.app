怎么保存藏品？六个环境要素决定藏品寿命

How to store a collection: 6 environmental factors

2026-02-08 · 6 分钟阅读6 min read · 古董集编辑整理Gudong editorial
BEGINNER / 入门指南 BEGINNER / Guide 怎么保存藏品？ How to store a collection 六个环境要素 6 environmental factors
藏

藏品买回来只是开始，能不能保存好，才决定它几十年后是什么状态。温度、湿度、光照、灰尘、虫害、接触——六个要素，每一个都能毁掉一件藏品。

先说明：本文讲的是藏品保存的通用原则，不构成专业修复建议。具体藏品（书画、瓷器、铜器、牙角等）保存需求不同，建议咨询专业机构。

一、六个环境要素

每个要素都有“杀手级”的破坏力，也都有对应的防护方法。

温

温度

温度剧烈变化会让材质膨胀收缩，导致开裂、变形。

怎么做
  • 稳定在 18–22°C
  • 避免空调直吹、暖气烘烤
  • 避免频繁移动
湿

湿度

湿度过高生霉、生锈；湿度过低干裂、变形。湿度是藏品的头号杀手。

怎么做
  • 控制在 45–55% RH
  • 用湿度计监测
  • 用除湿机 / 加湿器调节
光

光照

紫外线会让书画褪色、纸绢脆化、牙角泛黄。阳光和射灯都是威胁。

怎么做
  • 避免阳光直射
  • 用防紫外线玻璃或窗帘
  • LED 射灯不要长期照射
尘

灰尘

灰尘会磨损表面、堵塞缝隙、吸附湿气。长期不清理，会侵蚀包浆。

怎么做
  • 用防尘罩、玻璃柜
  • 用软毛刷、软布轻清
  • 不要用湿布擦古物
虫

虫害

书画、竹木、牙角、老家具是虫害高发区。虫蛀一旦发生，几乎无法逆转。

怎么做
  • 用樟木箱、防虫纸
  • 定期检查、通风
  • 发现虫蛀立即隔离
触

接触

手上的汗、油、酸会侵蚀藏品表面。频繁触摸会磨损包浆、破坏锈色。

怎么做
  • 戴棉手套再上手
  • 不要用手直接摸
  • 不要对着藏品说话、咳嗽

二、六个要素速查表

要素 风险 建议
温度开裂、变形18–22°C，稳定
湿度生霉、生锈、干裂45–55% RH
光照褪色、脆化避免阳光、紫外
灰尘磨损、侵蚀防尘罩、软毛刷
虫害虫蛀、不可逆樟木、防虫纸
接触汗、油侵蚀戴棉手套
实战提醒：六个要素里，湿度和光照是最容易被忽略、也最容易造成不可逆伤害的两个。先把这两个控制好，就成功了一半。

三、不同藏品的保存重点

  1. 书画：防潮、防光、防虫，卷轴要定期松卷
  2. 瓷器：防震、防碰撞，展示柜要固定
  3. 铜器：防潮、防氯，避免用湿布擦
  4. 玉器：防摔、防高温，可定期盘玩
  5. 牙角：防干燥、防温差，避免阳光直射
  6. 钱币：防潮、防手摸，用硬币盒、干燥剂

四、常见误区

  • 误区一：用湿布擦古物——湿气会侵蚀
  • 误区二：用塑料袋密封——内部湿气排不出，反而发霉
  • 误区三：放在窗边展示——阳光和紫外线是杀手
  • 误区四：天天上手把玩——磨损包浆、伤表面

下一步可以做什么？

  • 买一个温湿度计，放在藏品展示区
  • 给每个藏品配好防尘罩、手套、干燥剂
  • 每季度做一次藏品检查，记录温湿度变化

Buying a piece is only the beginning. How well you store it decides what it looks like decades later. Temperature, humidity, light, dust, pests, contact — any one can ruin a piece.

Note: General principles, not professional restoration advice. Different materials (paintings, porcelain, bronze, ivory) need different care — consult a professional.

1. Six environmental factors

Each factor can be destructive, and each has a countermeasure.

T

Temperature

Rapid changes cause expansion and contraction, leading to cracks and warping.

What to do
  • Keep at 18–22°C
  • Avoid AC drafts, heaters
  • Avoid frequent moves
H

Humidity

High humidity grows mould and rust; low humidity dries and cracks. Humidity is the number one killer.

What to do
  • Keep at 45–55% RH
  • Monitor with a hygrometer
  • Use dehumidifier / humidifier
L

Light

UV fades paintings, brittles paper and silk, yellows ivory. Sunlight and spotlights are threats.

What to do
  • Avoid direct sunlight
  • UV-filter glass or curtains
  • Don't leave LED spots on long
D

Dust

Dust abrades surfaces, clogs crevices, absorbs moisture. Left alone, it erodes patina.

What to do
  • Dust covers, glass cases
  • Soft brush, soft cloth
  • Never wipe antiques with wet cloth
P

Pests

Paintings, bamboo, wood, ivory, and furniture are high-risk. Once pests strike, damage is nearly irreversible.

What to do
  • Camphor chest, pest paper
  • Regular checks, ventilation
  • Isolate immediately if found
C

Contact

Hand sweat, oil, acid erode surfaces. Frequent touching wears patina and rust.

What to do
  • Wear cotton gloves
  • Don't touch directly
  • Don't talk or cough on pieces

2. Quick reference

Factor Risk Advice
TemperatureCracks, warping18–22°C, stable
HumidityMould, rust, cracks45–55% RH
LightFading, brittlenessAvoid sunlight, UV
DustAbrasion, erosionCovers, soft brush
PestsIrreversible damageCamphor, pest paper
ContactSweat, oil erosionCotton gloves
Tip: Of the six, humidity and light are the most overlooked and the most irreversible. Control those two and you're halfway there.

3. Key focus by material

  1. Paintings: moisture, light, pests; loosen scrolls periodically
  2. Porcelain: shock, impact; secure display cases
  3. Bronze: moisture, chloride; avoid wet cloth
  4. Jade: drops, heat; regular handling OK
  5. Ivory: dryness, temperature swings; avoid sun
  6. Coins: moisture, hand contact; coin holders, desiccant

4. Common misconceptions

  • Wipe antiques with wet cloth — moisture erodes
  • Seal in plastic — traps moisture, mould grows
  • Display by a window — sunlight and UV kill
  • Handle daily — wears patina and surface
