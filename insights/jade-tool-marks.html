玉器断代：从“砣痕”看年代

Dating jade by tool marks

2026-02-04 · 6 分钟阅读6 min read · 古董集编辑整理Gudong editorial

砣

玉器断代，除了看形制、纹饰，还有一个“硬指标”：砣痕。砣痕是玉器加工时工具留下的痕迹，工具变了，痕迹也变了。这篇按时代讲清四种典型砣痕。

先说明：本文讲的是砣痕断代的基本思路，不是鉴定结论。高仿玉器仍需结合其他特征综合判断。

一、四种典型砣痕

不同时代用不同工具，留下不同痕迹。从新石器时代到清代，砣痕经历了从粗到精的过程。

01
新石器

原始砣痕

用石制或木制工具加解玉砂加工，痕迹粗犷、深浅不一，线条常不连贯。

特征
  • 线条粗、深浅不一
  • 边缘不规则
  • 常有打磨痕迹覆盖
常见器物
  • 红山文化玉器
  • 良渚文化玉器
02
商周

青铜砣痕

用青铜砣具加工，痕迹比新石器时代规整，线条开始出现平行、等距的特征。

特征
  • 线条规整、平行
  • 有轻微弧度
  • 常有“一面坡”效果
常见器物
  • 商代玉戈、玉琮
  • 西周玉璧、玉璜
03
战汉

铁砣痕

铁工具出现后，砣痕更加锋利、精细。线条流畅、有力度，常有“游丝毛雕”的效果。

特征
  • 线条细、锋利、流畅
  • 有“游丝毛雕”感
  • 打磨精细
常见器物
  • 战国龙纹玉佩
  • 汉代玉璧、玉衣片
04
明清

精工砣痕

明清用脚踏砣机，痕迹精细、均匀。线条工整，常见“阳刻”和“减地”效果。

特征
  • 线条精细、均匀
  • 工整、有规矩
  • 打磨光滑
常见器物
  • 明代玉带板
  • 清代玉山子、玉牌

二、四种砣痕对比表

时代 工具 痕迹特征 代表器物
新石器石/木 + 解玉砂粗犷、深浅不一红山、良渚
商周青铜砣规整、平行、一面坡玉戈、玉琮
战汉铁砣细、锋利、游丝毛雕龙纹佩、玉璧
明清脚踏砣机精细、均匀、工整玉带板、玉山子
实战提醒：砣痕只是断代的线索之一，不能单靠它下结论。要结合形制、纹饰、沁色、包浆一起看。

三、上手怎么看？

  1. 看线条：是粗犷还是精细，是平行还是不规则
  2. 看边缘：边缘有没有“一面坡”或“游丝毛雕”
  3. 看打磨：打磨程度如何，是否覆盖砣痕
  4. 看整体：砣痕是否与器型、纹饰一致

四、常见误区

  • 误区一：有砣痕就是老——砣痕可以后做
  • 误区二：砣痕越粗越老——不一定，要看整体
  • 误区三：砣痕细就是明清——战汉也有细砣痕
  • 误区四：只看砣痕就能断代——要结合其他特征

下一步可以做什么？

  • 去博物馆看不同时代的玉器，重点看砣痕
  • 用放大镜对比不同时代玉器的线条特征
  • 把每次看到的不同砣痕拍下来，做自己的对比库

Dating jade isn't only about shape and decoration — there's a "hard indicator": tool marks. Different eras used different tools, leaving different traces. Here are four typical patterns.

Note: This is about basic dating logic, not authentication. High-end fakes need other features checked together.

1. Four typical tool marks

Different eras used different tools, leaving different traces. From Neolithic to Qing, marks went from coarse to fine.

01
Neolithic

Primitive marks

Stone or wood tools with abrasive sand. Marks are coarse, uneven, lines often broken.

Features
  • Coarse, uneven lines
  • Irregular edges
  • Often covered by polishing
Common pieces
  • Hongshan culture jade
  • Liangzhu culture jade
02
Shang-Zhou

Bronze wheel marks

Bronze tools. Marks are more regular, with parallel and equidistant lines.

Features
  • Regular, parallel lines
  • Slight curve
  • "One-sided slope" effect
Common pieces
  • Shang jade ge, cong
  • Western Zhou bi, huang
03
Warring States–Han

Iron wheel marks

Iron tools made marks sharper and finer. Lines are fluent and strong, often "hair-strand carving".

Features
  • Fine, sharp, fluent lines
  • "Hair-strand" feel
  • Fine polishing
Common pieces
  • Warring States dragon pendants
  • Han bi, jade suit plaques
04
Ming–Qing

Fine wheel marks

Foot-powered wheels. Marks are fine and even. Lines are neat, often with raised or reduced carving.

Features
  • Fine, even lines
  • Neat and regular
  • Smooth polishing
Common pieces
  • Ming jade belt plaques
  • Qing jade mountains, plaques

2. Comparison

Period Tool Marks Examples
NeolithicStone/wood + sandCoarse, unevenHongshan, Liangzhu
Shang-ZhouBronze wheelRegular, parallelGe, cong
Warring States–HanIron wheelFine, sharpDragon pendants, bi
Ming–QingFoot-powered wheelFine, evenBelt plaques, mountains
Tip: Tool marks are only one clue. Combine with shape, decoration, stains, and patina.

3. How to look

  1. Lines: coarse or fine? parallel or irregular?
  2. Edges: any "one-sided slope" or "hair-strand"?
  3. Polish: how much? does it cover the marks?
  4. Overall: do the marks match the shape and decoration?

4. Common misconceptions

  • Has marks = old — marks can be faked
  • Coarser = older — not necessarily
  • Fine = Ming-Qing — Warring States–Han also fine
  • Marks alone date it — combine with other features
