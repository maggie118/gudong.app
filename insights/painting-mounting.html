书画“揭裱”和“原裱”差在哪

Re-mounted vs. original mounting

2026-02-06 · 5 分钟阅读5 min read · 古董圈编辑整理 · 未经授权禁止转载Gudong editorial · No reposting without permission
PITFALLS / 避坑手册 PITFALLS / Guide 书画“揭裱”和“原裱” Re-mounted vs. original mounting 裱工对价格的影响 How mounting affects value
裱

书画的“裱”，不只是装饰，是画的一部分。原裱保真、保价；揭裱伤画、伤价。这篇用分层拆解的方式，讲清裱工的门道。

先说明：本文讲的是原裱和揭裱的基本区别，不是鉴定结论。具体书画仍需结合纸、墨、印、款综合判断。

一、装裱的五层结构

一幅书画的装裱，从内到外有五层。每一层都有讲究。

1
画心

画心（原作）

画家实际作画的部分。纸、绢、墨、色都在这一层。画心是价值的核心，其他四层都是为它服务。

2
命纸

命纸（托心纸）

直接托在画心背面的纸，是画心的“命”。命纸如果被揭掉，画心就伤。原裱命纸完好，是书画完整性的关键。

3
覆背

覆背纸

在命纸之后加的加固层，通常两到三层。覆背纸的作用是固定画心、防潮、防虫。覆背纸是否原装，是判断是否揭裱的重要依据。

4
镶料

镶料（边框）

画心四周的绫、绢边框。镶料的颜色、材质、样式，反映时代风格。原裱镶料通常与画心时代一致。

5
天地杆

天杆、地杆、轴头

最外层的挂杆、卷轴、轴头。天杆挂、地杆卷，轴头是卷轴两端的装饰。原裱的天杆、地杆、轴头通常用料考究。

二、原裱 vs 揭裱

原裱 保真

  • 命纸：完好，与画心一体
  • 覆背：原装，有自然老化
  • 镶料：与画心时代一致
  • 天地杆：用料考究，有包浆
  • 价值：保真、保价，是加分项

揭裱 伤画

  • 命纸：被揭掉，画心变薄
  • 覆背：新换，无自然老化
  • 镶料：可能新配，与画心不符
  • 天地杆：可能后配，缺乏包浆
  • 价值：伤画、伤价，除非修复必要

三、对比表

特征 原裱 揭裱
命纸完好被揭掉
覆背原装、自然老化新换、无老化
镶料与画心一致可能新配
天地杆原装、有包浆可能后配
价格影响加分减分
实战提醒：书画的“原裱”是加分项，不只是装饰。买书画时，要问清楚是不是原裱，有没有揭裱记录。

四、上手怎么看？

  1. 看命纸：是否完好，有没有被揭过的痕迹
  2. 看覆背：是否有自然老化，还是新的
  3. 看镶料：颜色、材质、样式是否与画心一致
  4. 看天地杆：是否有包浆，还是后配的
  5. 问来源：问清楚有没有揭裱记录

五、常见误区

  • 误区一：裱得新就是好——新裱可能是揭裱
  • 误区二：揭裱是修复——揭裱伤画，不是所有画都该揭
  • 误区三：原裱就是旧——原裱不一定旧，关键是完整性
  • 误区四：看画就够了——裱工也是画的一部分

下一步可以做什么？

  • 去博物馆看原裱书画，记住命纸、覆背、镶料的感觉
  • 找一件有揭裱记录的书画，对比着看
  • 买书画时，把是否原裱作为必问项

A painting's mounting isn't just decoration — it's part of the work. Original mounting preserves authenticity and value; re-mounting damages both. Here's a layer-by-layer breakdown.

Note: This is about basic differences, not authentication. Final judgement needs paper, ink, seal, and signature checked together.

1. Five layers of mounting

From inside out, a painting has five layers. Each matters.

1
Core

Painting core

The actual painted area. Paper, silk, ink, colour all here. The core is the value; the other four layers serve it.

2
Lining

Lining paper

The paper directly behind the core — the core's "life". If removed, the core is damaged. An intact lining is key to integrity.

3
Backing

Backing paper

Reinforcement behind the lining, usually two or three layers. Fixes the core, resists moisture and insects. Original backing is a key sign of original mounting.

4
Border

Border materials

Silk or damask borders around the core. Colour, material, and style reflect the period. Original borders usually match the core's period.

5
Rods

Top rod, bottom roller, knobs

Outer hanging rod, rolling rod, and end knobs. Original rods and knobs are usually well made.

2. Original vs re-mounted

Original Authentic

  • Lining: intact, bonded to core
  • Backing: original, naturally aged
  • Border: matches core's period
  • Rods: well made, patinated
  • Value: preserves authenticity and value

Re-mounted Damages

  • Lining: removed, core thinned
  • Backing: replaced, no ageing
  • Border: possibly new, mismatched
  • Rods: possibly replaced, no patina
  • Value: damages painting and price

3. Comparison

Feature Original Re-mounted
LiningIntactRemoved
BackingOriginal, agedNew, no ageing
BorderMatches corePossibly new
RodsOriginal, patinatedPossibly replaced
PriceAdds valueReduces value
Tip: Original mounting is a plus, not just decoration. When buying, always ask whether it's original and whether it's been re-mounted.

4. How to look

  1. Lining: intact? any sign of removal?
  2. Backing: naturally aged, or new?
  3. Border: colour, material, style match the core?
  4. Rods: patinated, or replaced?
  5. Ask: any re-mounting history?

5. Common misconceptions

  • Fresh mounting = good — fresh may mean re-mounted
  • Re-mounting = restoration — it damages; not all paintings should be re-mounted
  • Original = old — original isn't necessarily old; integrity is what matters
  • Just look at the painting — mounting is part of the work
