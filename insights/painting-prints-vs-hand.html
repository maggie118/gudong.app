书画“印刷品”和“手绘”怎么分？放大镜看这 3 处

Prints vs. hand-painted — 3 checks with a loupe

2026-02-02 · 5 分钟阅读5 min read · 古董圈编辑整理 · 未经授权禁止转载Gudong editorial · No reposting without permission

画

书画市场上，印刷品冒充手绘很常见。远看颜色一样，放大看差别就出来了。这篇讲三个关键观察点：网点、墨色、笔触。

先说明：本文讲的是印刷品和手绘的基本区别，不是鉴定结论。高仿印刷品或木版水印仍需专业鉴定。

三个关键观察点

01

看网点

印刷品是用网点拼出颜色的。用 10 倍放大镜看，会看到规则排列的小点。手绘没有网点，颜色是连续的、有浓淡变化。

手绘
  • 颜色连续，无规则小点
  • 浓淡自然过渡
  • 放大后看到笔触
印刷品
  • 可见规则网点
  • 颜色是点拼成的
  • 放大后是印刷结构
02

看墨色

手绘的墨色是渗入纸或绢的，有层次、有渗化。印刷品的墨色浮在表面，颜色均匀，缺少自然变化。

手绘
  • 墨色渗入纸绢
  • 有浓淡、有渗化
  • 背面有墨透
印刷品
  • 墨色浮于表面
  • 颜色均匀、无渗化
  • 背面无墨透
03

看笔触

手绘有笔触，起笔、收笔、转折都有笔锋。印刷品没有真实笔触，只有网点的边缘。

手绘
  • 有起笔、收笔、笔锋
  • 转折有力度变化
  • 线条有粗细变化
印刷品
  • 无真实笔触
  • 线条边缘整齐
  • 无粗细变化

三个观察点速查表

观察点 手绘 印刷品
网点无有规则网点
墨色渗入纸绢，有层次浮于表面，均匀
笔触有笔锋、有变化无笔触
背面可能有墨透通常无墨透
实战提醒：10 倍放大镜看网点，是最直接的判断方法。但要注意：木版水印是半手工的，也有笔触感，需要更专业的判断。

上手怎么看？

  1. 先看整体：远看颜色是否过于均匀、缺少变化
  2. 再看网点：用 10 倍放大镜看有无规则网点
  3. 看墨色：是否渗入纸绢，背面有没有墨透
  4. 看笔触：线条是否有起笔、收笔、粗细变化

常见误区

  • 误区一：看着像就是手绘——现在高仿印刷品很像
  • 误区二：有颜色就是手绘——印刷品也有颜色
  • 误区三：有笔触就是手绘——木版水印也有笔触感
  • 误区四：看落款就够了——落款也要看是不是后加

下一步可以做什么？

  • 买一个 10 倍放大镜，随身携带
  • 找一张确定的印刷品和一张手绘，对比着看
  • 把每次看到的不同样本拍下来，做自己的对比库

In the painting market, prints passed off as hand-painted are common. They look the same from afar, but differ under magnification. Three key checks: halftone dots, ink, and brushwork.

Note: This is about basic differences, not authentication. High-end prints or woodblock watermarks still need professional judgement.

Three key checks

01

Check for halftone dots

Prints build colour from dots. Under a 10x loupe, you'll see a regular pattern of small dots. Hand-painted works have no dots — colour is continuous and graded.

Hand-painted
  • Continuous colour, no dots
  • Natural gradation
  • Brushwork visible under loupe
Print
  • Regular halftone dots
  • Colour built from dots
  • Printed structure under loupe
02

Check the ink

Hand-painted ink sinks into paper or silk, with layers and bleed. Print ink sits on the surface, uniform and without natural variation.

Hand-painted
  • Ink sinks into paper/silk
  • Gradation and bleed
  • Show-through on reverse
Print
  • Ink on surface
  • Uniform, no bleed
  • No show-through
03

Check the brushwork

Hand-painted works show brush entry, exit, and turns. Prints have no real brushwork — only dot edges.

Hand-painted
  • Entry, exit, brush tip
  • Turns show pressure changes
  • Line width varies
Print
  • No real brushwork
  • Lines edge-clean
  • No width variation

Quick reference

Check Hand-painted Print
DotsNoneRegular dots
InkSinks in, layeredOn surface, uniform
BrushworkTip, variationNone
ReverseMay show throughUsually none
Tip: A 10x loupe for dots is the most direct check. But note: woodblock watermarks are semi-handmade and show brushwork — they need more professional judgement.

How to look

  1. Overall: is the colour too uniform from afar?
  2. Dots: 10x loupe for a regular dot pattern
  3. Ink: does it sink into the paper? reverse show-through?
  4. Brushwork: entry, exit, width variation

Common misconceptions

  • Looks hand-painted = hand-painted — high-end prints are close
  • Colour = hand-painted — prints have colour too
  • Brushwork = hand-painted — woodblock watermarks show brushwork
  • Just check the signature — check whether it's added later
