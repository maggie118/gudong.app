清代民窑青花怎么分辨年代？3 个上手要点

Dating Qing folk kiln blue-and-white: 3 hands-on signals

2026-02-18 · 6 分钟阅读6 min read · 古董圈编辑整理 · 未经授权禁止转载Gudong editorial · No reposting without permission
BEGINNER / 入门指南 BEGINNER / Guide 摊主说是“清中期”，你拿起来看这3个地方就知道真假 Dating Qing folk kiln blue-and-white 釉面 · 胎质 · 发色 Glaze · Body · Pigment tone
青

很多刚入门的藏家会问：拿到一只青花碗，能不能不送检，就大致判断它属于清代哪个时期？

可以。对清代民窑青花来说，釉面、胎质、发色这三项，已经能帮你把大部分常见器物放进一个合理的时间区间。

先说明：本文讲的是“断代感觉”，不是“真伪鉴定”。民窑青花存世量大、仿品也多，任何单一特征都不能定论。

一、釉面：先看“亮”还是“润”

  • 顺治至康熙早期：釉色多青白，釉面较厚，光泽偏硬。
  • 康熙中晚期：釉面温润，俗称“紧皮亮釉”。
  • 雍正、乾隆：釉面更细腻均匀，白度提高。
  • 嘉庆以后：釉层趋薄，光泽变弱，部分出现“波浪釉”。

二、胎质：重点看底足露胎

康熙民窑胎土淘洗较细，露胎处白中带一点糯；乾隆以后部分民窑胎土含杂质增多。康熙常见二层台底、泥鳅背；晚清常见平底、斜削足。

三、发色：青花的“蓝”里藏时间

  • 康熙：翠蓝、层次分明，有“墨分五色”的分水效果。
  • 雍正、乾隆：柔和稳定，蓝中带灰。
  • 嘉庆至道光：蓝中泛灰更明显，部分发色漂浮。
  • 晚清民国：洋蓝，发色艳而单薄，泛紫。
实战提醒：“分水”层次丰富、蓝得沉而不飘的，先往康熙、雍正方向想；蓝得发艳发紫、平涂无层次的，多半偏晚清民国。

三项要合起来看

釉面温润、底足二层台、发色翠蓝带分水，大概率落在康熙中晚期至雍正。釉面偏薄泛白、底足斜削、发色洋蓝泛紫，则更可能是晚清民国。

New collectors often ask: can you roughly date a blue-and-white bowl without thermoluminescence?

Yes. For Qing folk kiln blue-and-white, three basics — glaze, body, pigment tone — can place most common pieces in a reasonable time range.

Note: This is about dating intuition, not authentication. Folk kiln wares survive in large numbers, and copies are common; no single feature is decisive.

1. Glaze: bright or soft?

  • Shunzhi–early Kangxi: bluish-white, thicker, harder sheen.
  • Mid–late Kangxi: softer, known as "tight skin, bright glaze".
  • Yongzheng–Qianlong: finer and whiter.
  • Jiaqing onward: thinner glaze, weaker sheen, sometimes "wave glaze".

2. Body: read the exposed foot

Kangxi folk kiln bodies are finely washed, with exposed clay white and slightly rice-like; after Qianlong, some bodies contain more impurities. Kangxi often shows a two-tier foot and eel-back; late Qing often has a flat base and slanted-cut foot.

3. Pigment tone: the blue tells time

  • Kangxi: crisp blue, layered, with "five shades of ink" shading.
  • Yongzheng–Qianlong: softer and stable, grey-blue.
  • Jiaqing–Daoguang: more obvious grey, some floating colour.
  • Late Qing–Republic: "foreign blue", bright and thin, purplish.
Tip: Rich layered shading and deep, settled blue point to Kangxi / Yongzheng; bright purplish blue with flat wash points to late Qing / Republic.

Combine all three

Soft glaze + two-tier foot + layered cobalt → mid–late Kangxi to Yongzheng. Thin whitish glaze + slanted foot + purplish blue → late Qing–Republic.
