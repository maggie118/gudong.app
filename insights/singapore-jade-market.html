新加坡玉器市场：去哪看、怎么看

Singapore jade market: where to look, how to look

2026-01-30 · 6 分钟阅读6 min read · 古董集编辑整理Gudong editorial
MARKET / 市场观察 MARKET / Guide 新加坡玉器市场 Singapore jade market 玉器水深 · 渠道比眼力更重要 Jade is deep · channels matter more than eye
玉

玉器比瓷器水更深：B+C 翡翠、染色和田玉、合成材料……渠道不对，眼力再好也可能翻车。这篇按区域讲清新加坡看玉的四个去处。

先说明：本文是新加坡本地玉器购买渠道的概览，不构成投资建议。具体店铺、拍卖行请自行核实营业信息。

四个主要区域

A
传统玉器街 · 牛车水一带
Chinatown · 传统老店

牛车水一带有不少经营多年的玉器老店，货品从低端到中高端都有。老店看口碑，新店看证书。

优点
  • 店铺集中，方便对比
  • 老店有信誉积累
  • 价格弹性大，可议价
小心
  • 部分店 B+C 混卖
  • 证书真伪要看机构
  • 游客店价格虚高
B
商场珠宝专柜 · 乌节路
Orchard · 品牌保障

乌节路各大商场的珠宝专柜，货品以现代翡翠首饰为主。价格透明、证书齐全，但溢价也高。

优点
  • 证书齐全、真伪有保障
  • 明码标价、售后完善
  • 适合新手建立标准
小心
  • 溢价高，不是投资首选
  • 老玉、古玉几乎没有
  • 销售话术多
C
拍卖行 · 本地与区域
Auction · 高端渠道

新加坡本地及区域拍卖行，偶尔会有玉器专场。预展是最好的上手学习机会，能看到不同等级的真品。

优点
  • 来源相对清晰
  • 预展可免费上手
  • 高端货集中
小心
  • 图录描述 ≠ 实物
  • 佣金加价后总价高
  • 高古玉有来源争议
D
藏家社群 · 私下交易
Collector Circles · 圈内

通过协会、微信群、拍卖活动认识本地藏家。信息最真实，但需要时间建立信任，不适合着急买的人。

优点
  • 价格通常更实在
  • 能学到真实经验
  • 货源稳定
小心
  • “圈内价”也有水分
  • 没有证书的货要谨慎
  • 人情难拒绝

四个区域对比表

区域 价格 风险 适合
牛车水老店中-高中有经验
乌节路专柜高低新手建标准
拍卖行中-高低-中进阶
藏家社群中中长期玩家
实战提醒：玉器看实物比看图重要 10 倍。买之前一定要看证书（且看机构是否权威），最好带懂行的人一起去。

怎么用这四个区域？

  1. 先建标准：去乌节路专柜看真品，建立颜色的手感
  2. 再练眼力：去牛车水老店多看多问，不急着买
  3. 再看高端：去拍卖预展上手高端真品
  4. 最后入圈：加入藏家社群，长期积累

常见误区

  • 误区一：有证书就是 A 货——证书要看机构
  • 误区二：商场就贵——商场也有高性价比
  • 误区三：拍卖行不会卖假——拍卖行也走眼
  • 误区四：熟人不会骗——熟人也要看东西

下一步可以做什么？

  • 周末去乌节路专柜，只看不买，建立标准
  • 去牛车水老店对比不同价位玉器的差异
  • 查一下近期拍卖预展，去上手高端真品

Jade is deeper than porcelain: B+C jadeite, dyed hetian, synthetic materials. Wrong channel, and even a good eye can fail. Here are four places to see jade in Singapore.

Note: An overview of local channels, not investment advice. Verify shop and auction details yourself.

Four main zones

A
Chinatown jade shops
Traditional shops

Several long-standing jade shops around Chinatown, from low to mid-high end. Old shops trade on reputation; new shops on certificates.

Pros
  • Concentrated, easy to compare
  • Old shops have track record
  • Room to negotiate
Watch
  • Some mix in B+C
  • Check the certifying lab
  • Tourist shops overprice
B
Orchard mall counters
Brand protection

Jewellery counters in Orchard malls, mostly modern jadeite. Transparent pricing, full certificates, but with a premium.

Pros
  • Full certificates, safe
  • Fixed prices, good after-sales
  • Good for beginners
Watch
  • High premium, not investment
  • Almost no antique jade
  • Sales talk
C
Auction houses
Local and regional

Local and regional auction houses occasionally hold jade sales. Previews are the best free classroom for handling real pieces.

Pros
  • Clearer provenance
  • Free previews
  • High-end concentrated
Watch
  • Catalogue ≠ reality
  • Buyer's premium adds up
  • Antique jade provenance issues
D
Collector circles
Private deals

Meet local collectors via associations, chat groups, auction events. Honest information, but takes time to build trust.

Pros
  • Usually fairer prices
  • Real experience shared
  • Stable supply
Watch
  • "Inside price" isn't always good
  • Be careful without certificates
  • Hard to refuse a friend

Zone comparison

Zone Price Risk Suits
Chinatown shopsMed-HighMediumExperienced
Orchard countersHighLowBeginners
Auction housesMed-HighLow-MedAdvanced
Collector circlesMediumMediumLong-term
Tip: For jade, seeing in person matters 10x more than photos. Always check the certificate (and the lab), and bring someone knowledgeable.

How to use them

  1. Build standard: Orchard counters for real reference
  2. Practice: Chinatown shops, look and ask, don't rush
  3. Go high-end: auction previews for top pieces
  4. Enter the circle: collector groups, long term

Common misconceptions

  • Certificate = A grade — check the lab
  • Malls are always expensive — sometimes fair
  • Auction = no fakes — they miss too
  • Friends = safe — check the piece anyway
