🛡️卖家入驻指南Seller Onboarding Guide

一、基础发布 · 种子会员前2件免费
1. Basic Listing · First 2 free for Founder Members
藏品进入「最新上架」免费流量池。创始会员（早期入驻卖家）享有前2件藏品免费发布额度；第3件藏品起，每件每年 SGD 20。服务按年有效期，到期自动失效。不开通展馆也可以正常发布藏品。

→ 前往发布藏品
Listings enter the free "New Listings" pool. Founder Members (early‑admission dealers) receive complimentary listing for their first 2 items. Starting from the 3rd item: SGD 20 per item per‑year. Service expires automatically after 1‑year term. You may list items without purchasing a dealer‑gallery.

→ List your item
二、付费置顶（今日发现插槽）· 每7天：SGD 180
2. Paid Pin Slot (Today's Finds) · Per 7‑day cycle: SGD 180
将合格藏品放置首页「今日发现」栏目置顶插槽；平台同时仅开放1‑2个位置，先到先得。图片、年代、品类信息必须完整，不合格藏品不可购买。服务有效期7天，到期系统自动取消置顶，恢复普通藏品展示。仅提升曝光，不代表平台鉴定保真。
Place eligible listing inside homepage "Today's Finds" pinned slot. Only 1‑2 concurrent slots available, first‑come‑first‑served. Complete photos, era and category data required. Term lasts 7‑days; pin status auto‑revokes upon expiry. Exposure boost does not constitute platform authentication.
三、精选展示（编辑精选）· 每件每年：SGD 80
3. Featured Listing (Editor's Picks) · Per item / year: SGD 80
登上首页独立「编辑精选」付费栏目，带编辑精选徽章，优先排序。与今日发现置顶属于相互独立服务。有效期1年，到期自动退出精选栏目。
Appear in dedicated homepage "Editor's Picks" section with badge and priority ordering. Separate service from Today's Finds pin. Valid for one‑year; auto‑removed after expiry.
四、卖家展馆 · 标准展馆年费：SGD 380
4. Dealer Gallery · Standard Gallery Annual Fee: SGD 380
付费开通后，获得独立专属展馆静态页面（独立分享URL，支持搜索引擎收录），集中展示该卖家全部在售藏品，同时出现在卖家展馆公开列表。展馆服务有效期1年，到期展馆页面自动提示到期，藏品不再对外展示。

可叠加增值：优先展馆 +SGD 220/年（展馆列表置顶、金边卡片）；馆内置顶藏品每件 SGD 60/年。
→ 浏览卖家展馆列表
Upon purchase you receive dedicated static gallery‑page with shareable URL for search‑engine indexing to showcase all your active inventory, visible in public dealer‑gallery directory. Gallery licence valid 1‑year; gallery page shows expiry notice and hides inventory after expiration.

Optional add‑ons: Priority Gallery +SGD 220/yr (directory pin, gold‑border card); In‑gallery pinned‑item SGD 60/item/yr.
→ Browse dealer‑galleries directory
重要业务说明：基础发布、付费置顶（今日发现插槽）、精选展示（编辑精选）、卖家展馆四项服务互相独立，购买其中一项不会自动包含其余服务。一件藏品可同时购买多项推广。到期前需要主动续费；不续费则系统自动降级失效。付款支持 PayNow / 银行转账，平台提供收款收据。

免责：平台仅提供线上信息曝光服务，不介入买卖双方实际交易，不做真伪担保，不参与资金收付。
Important terms: Basic Listing, Today's Finds pin, Editor's Picks feature and Dealer‑Gallery are four independent services; purchasing one does not include others. One listing may carry multiple promotions. Renew before expiry; services auto‑downgrade without renewal. Payments via PayNow / bank transfer; receipts will be issued.

Disclaimer: Platform provides digital exposure service only. We do not mediate physical transactions, authenticate objects or handle funds.
