平台流程
Platform Flow

卖家完整业务流程

Full Seller Workflow

  1. 填写【卖家入驻申请】表单提交基础资料，等待平台人工审核；
  2. Complete the seller-onboarding application form and wait for manual review;
  3. 审核通过，平台分配唯一 Seller ID（卖家编号）；审核不通过将邮件告知原因；
  4. Once approved, you receive your unique Seller ID. Rejection reasons will be sent via email;
  5. 拿到 Seller ID 之后，使用【发布藏品】表单提交藏品资料；表单提交只是刊登申请，藏品不会立刻对外上线；
  6. With valid Seller ID, submit item details via listing form. Submission is only an application; listings are NOT published automatically.
  7. 平台后台审核藏品内容，核对刊登服务费用：创始卖家消耗免费额度 / 普通卖家确认收款；
  8. Platform reviews item content and verifies listing-service payment / complimentary credits for Founder-Dealers;
  9. 审核与费用确认全部完成后，藏品才对外公开展示；到期藏品会被手动下架。
  10. Item goes public only after review and payment-verification. Expired listings will be taken offline manually.

提示：未完成入驻审核的账号，无法提交藏品刊登。提交藏品表单时务必填写正确 Seller ID。

Note: Un-approved applicants cannot submit listings. Please provide correct Seller ID inside the listing form.

平台流程
Platform Flow

买家完整业务流程

Full Buyer Workflow

  1. 浏览平台藏品库、卖家展馆，筛选感兴趣藏品；查看报价方式（价格区间 / 一口价 / 私聊）；
  2. Browse public listings and dealer-galleries, filter antiques of interest, check pricing mode (range / fixed / private-inquiry);
  3. 直接联系卖家，自行议价、确认货品细节、来源、交付、付款与运输安排；平台不介入交易；
  4. Contact sellers directly to negotiate price, item details, provenance, payment and shipping. Platform will not get involved in transactions;
  5. 需要第三方参考意见：可购买【专家咨询】服务获取顾问书面参考意见（不构成法律鉴定）；
  6. For third-party reference: purchase Expert Consultation for consultant remarks (not formal legal appraisal);
  7. 找不到目标藏品：可提交需求购买【委托找货】服务，平台帮你匹配卖家藏品资源；
  8. If you cannot locate target antiques: use Sourcing Service and we match potential inventory for you;
  9. 全部交易、转账、物流、风险核验，均由买卖双方自行负责。
  10. All payments, shipping and risk-verification are handled solely between buyer and seller.
