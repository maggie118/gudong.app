为什么不是 699 ÷ 30 = S$23.30/件？Why isn't it 699 ÷ 30 = S$23.30 per item?
S$699 不是 30 件商品的“刊登费”。它买的是一个年度数字资产：你的名字、你的藏馆、你的独立网址、你的藏品展示空间。这是为你的收藏建立一个专属的线上藏馆，而不是按件计价的发布服务。S$699 is not a "listing fee" for 30 items. It buys a year-long digital asset: your name, your gallery, your dedicated URL, your display space. It is a dedicated online gallery built for your collection, not a pay-per-item publishing service.
我为什么现在就要买 S$699？Why buy S$699 right now?
如果你只是偶尔卖一件，S$59 的帮我刊登就足够。如果你准备持续经营自己的收藏展示，S$699 帮助你建立长期的线上藏馆，沉淀你的收藏资产。S$59 是一次性解决问题，S$699 是建立长期资产。If you only sell occasionally, S$59 List-For-Me is enough. If you plan to continuously develop your collection showcase, S$699 helps you build a lasting online gallery and accumulate your collection assets. S$59 solves a one-off need; S$699 builds a long-term asset.

平台为什么不收取交易佣金？Why doesn't the platform charge a sales commission?
古董圈属于藏品信息展示与撮合平台，不介入藏品实际交易流程，不代为保管、代收买卖货款，因此不会针对成交金额抽取佣金。平台全部收入来自线上藏馆订阅、首页推荐、协助上架等展示类服务费。买卖双方自行协商成交价格，交易金额和平台无关。Gudong is a listing display and matchmaking platform. It does not step into the actual transaction process, does not hold items or collect payments on behalf of either party, and therefore takes no commission on sale amounts. All platform revenue comes from display-based services such as gallery subscriptions, homepage features and listing assistance. Buyers and sellers negotiate the price themselves; the transaction amount has nothing to do with the platform.
