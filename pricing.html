Pricing, Answered

平台为什么不收取交易佣金？
Why doesn't the platform charge a transaction commission?
古董集是信息展示与匹配平台，不代收藏品交易货款、不参与买卖流程，因此不针对成交本身抽成。平台收入完全来自刊登、私家藏馆、首页推荐等展示与曝光类服务费，买卖双方私下议价、成交的金额与平台无关。
Gudong is a discovery and matching platform — it does not collect sale proceeds or take part in the transaction, so it does not take a cut of what an item actually sells for. Revenue comes entirely from display and exposure services (listing, gallery, homepage feature); the price buyer and seller privately agree on is not the platform's business.
「委托找货」的信息对接费算佣金吗？
Is the information-matching fee on the Sourcing Service a commission?
不算。委托找货是买家自愿购买的独立增值服务，用于人工找货、匹配与约谈，不购买它完全不影响正常浏览、联系卖家和自行成交。信息对接费是固定金额，对应的是人工服务本身，不随成交价浮动。
No. The Sourcing Service is an optional add-on for manual sourcing, matching and introductions — skipping it has no effect on free browsing, contacting sellers, or completing a deal on your own. The matching fee is a fixed amount for that manual work, not a percentage of the sale price.
