Questions about pricing & services

平台为什么不收取交易佣金？
Why does the platform not charge transaction commission?
古董圈属于藏品信息展示与撮合平台，不介入藏品实际交易流程，不代为保管、代收买卖货款，因此不会针对成交金额抽取佣金。平台全部收入来自线上藏馆订阅、首页推荐、协助上架等展示类服务费。买卖双方自行协商成交价格，交易金额和平台无关。
Gudong is an artefact discovery and introduction platform. We do not take part in physical transactions nor hold buyer‑seller payments, hence no commission on sales. All platform revenue comes from display‑related services such as gallery subscriptions, homepage features and assisted listings. Sale prices are agreed privately between parties and independent of the platform.
委托找货的信息对接费属于交易佣金吗？
Is the matching‑introduction fee for sourcing a sales commission?
不属于。委托找货是买家自愿选购的独立增值服务，用于人工检索藏品、匹配卖家、代为沟通约谈。即使不购买该服务，也可以免费浏览平台藏品、联系卖家、自行完成交易。信息对接费为固定服务费，不会跟随藏品成交价按比例浮动。
No. Sourcing Service is an optional paid add‑on for manual research, seller matching and introductions. You can browse listings and contact sellers for free without purchasing this service. The matching‑introduction fee is a flat service charge and not a percentage‑based sales commission.
已经支付的平台服务费可以申请退款吗？
Am I eligible for a refund for fees already paid?
Collector Launch Kit年度订阅、首页推荐服务开通之后，原则上不予退款。若因平台故障造成服务完全不可用，可联系客服核实处理。委托找货、专家视频咨询退款规则以对应服务描述为准。
Once activated, Collector Launch Kit annual subscription and homepage feature slots are generally non‑refundable. Contact support only if platform‑side failure renders service completely unavailable. Refund terms for sourcing and expert‑consult services follow their respective service descriptions.
付款完成之后，服务多久可以生效？
How quickly will my service activate after payment?
PayNow或者银行转账完成，平台核实到账后，一般1个工作日内开通服务；周末与公共节假日会顺延至下一个工作日处理。
After PayNow or bank‑transfer payment is verified, services are normally activated within one business day. Requests received over weekends or public holidays will be processed on the next working day.
创始卖家88个名额如何统计，创始卖家包含哪些权益？
How are the 88 Founding‑Seller seats counted and what benefits are included?
名额以卖家申请审核通过时间排序统计。截至2026年10月1日，已有五位卖家入驻，实时剩余席位：83。创始卖家权益：2件免费刊登额度（1年有效期）、永久创始卖家标识、藏家启动包续费特惠价、新功能优先体验、优先审核与客服、专属曝光机会、未来新增卖家权益优先享有。
Seats are counted by the time of successful application approval. As of 1 October 2026, five sellers have been approved, remaining seats: 83. Founding‑seller benefits: two complimentary one‑year listing credits, permanent founding‑seller badge, discounted renewal for Collector Launch Kit, early‑access to new features, priority review & support, exclusive exposure slots and priority access to future seller benefits.
