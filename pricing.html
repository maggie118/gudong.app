S$599 的私人藏馆年费，值得投入吗？Is the S$599 annual fee for the Private Gallery worth it?
这不是简单的图片存储费用，是投资您在收藏圈的个人声誉与数字阵地。创始藏家享受特惠价 S$599（非创始藏家 S$799），按一年折算每天约 S$1.6，换来独立藏家主页、圈层曝光、人工管家协助以及 0% 佣金，适合有一定藏品、希望建立个人收藏形象的资深藏家。如果只是偶尔单件发布藏品，精品扩容层的 S$59 单件刊登会更适合。This is not simply an image storage fee — it is an investment in your personal reputation and digital presence in the collecting world. Founding collectors enjoy the special price of S$599 (regular S$799), about S$1.6/day, for a dedicated collector homepage, community exposure, concierge assistance and 0% commission. It suits established collectors who wish to build a personal collection identity. If you only list items occasionally, the S$59 single listing is a better fit.
