Q: 我该上传什么格式和尺寸的图片？文件不能超过多大？ Q: What image format and size should I upload? Is there a file size limit?
A: 请上传 JPG 或 PNG 格式的图片。请注意，平台系统要求单张图片大小不能超过 50KB。建议您在手机上选择“中等质量”或“压缩”后上传，以确保系统能顺利接收。 A: Please upload images in JPG or PNG format. Please note that the system requires each image to be strictly under 50KB. We recommend compressing the images on your phone before uploading to ensure successful submission.
Q: 为什么免费基础陈列只能传 3 张图，而专业上架能传 5 张？ Q: Why does Basic Display allow 3 photos, while Professional allows 5?
A: 中高端藏品的展示需要多角度呈现。专业上架提供的 5 张图（正面、背面、上面、底部、细节）是行家看货的基本标准。免费基础陈列作为体验，只提供 3 个基础展示位。 A: Showcasing mid-to-high-end antiques requires multiple angles. The 5 photos in Professional Listing (front, back, top, base, detail) represent the industry standard for serious buyers. Free Basic Display offers 3 slots as a trial.
Q: “早鸟价”和“全价”有什么区别？什么时候会涨价？ Q: What's the difference between "Early-bird" and "Full price"? When will prices increase?
A: S$288（专业上架）和 S$588（私家藏馆）是专门给第二批「先锋藏家」的早鸟价。名额满 50 人或 2026 年 12 月 31 日后，新申请将分别恢复至 S$488 和 S$1,288 的全价。在期限内开通的先锋藏家可永久锁定折扣——指首次购买及后续每一次续费，同一藏家、同一服务项均按创始价；身份须维持中高端藏品标准与基本活跃度（详见下方「先锋藏家」说明）。 A: S$288 (Professional) and S$588 (Gallery) are early-bird rates for the second batch, the Pioneer Members. After 50 slots are filled or by 31 Dec 2026, new applications revert to S$488 and S$1,288. Those who join before the deadline keep the founding rate permanently — meaning the same collector pays the founding price on the first purchase and on every renewal, for the same service, provided the account stays within our mid-to-high-end standard and remains reasonably active (see the Pioneer Member terms below).
Q: 平台会提供鉴定证书吗？会帮我看真假吗？ Q: Does the platform provide authentication certificates or verify authenticity?
A: 平台不出具具有法律效力的“鉴定证书”，也不承担真伪担保责任。但我们的资深团队会在审核时，为您提供顾问个人意见（如断代参考、品相点评）。该意见仅供买卖双方交流参考，最终真伪判定与交易决策需由买家自行负责。 A: The platform does not issue legally binding "authentication certificates" and does not assume liability for authenticity. However, our senior team may provide personal advisory opinions (e.g., dating references, condition notes) during review. These opinions are for reference only; final judgment and transaction decisions rest with the buyer.
Q: 如果我有更多图片或需要实物摄影怎么办？ Q: What if I have more photos or need physical photography?
A: 基础套餐内已包含标准数量的图片优化。如果您有特殊需求（如增加瑕疵特写、底款特写，或需要专业摄影师上门拍摄），请直接点击 WhatsApp 联系客服，我们会按需报价。 A: Standard packages include the listed number of photo optimizations. For special requests (e.g., extra close-ups of flaws or marks, or on-site photography), please contact us on WhatsApp for a custom quote.
Q: 我能知道有多少人在看我的藏品吗？ Q: Can I see how many people viewed my pieces?
A: 可以。开通私家藏馆后，您的藏家收件箱顶部会显示「近 30 天 · N 人浏览 · M 人收藏 / 关注」。这是去重后的匿名汇总数字：我们只统计「有多少人看过、多少人收藏」，不会告诉您他们是谁，也不会把买家的姓名、邮箱或号码给到藏家。未开通藏馆的藏家不显示该项。 A: Yes. Once your Private Gallery is active, your inbox shows "last 30 days · N visitors · M saves / follows". These are de-duplicated anonymous counts: how many people looked and how many saved — never who they are, and buyer names, emails or numbers are never shared with collectors. The stat is not shown for non-gallery collectors.
