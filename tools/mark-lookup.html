瓷器底款快查 Porcelain Mark & Seal Lookup
看瓷器认不出字、分不清官民窑、辨不准年代？按明清年号一键查标准款识文字、款识形态、发色与伪款避坑要点。 Can't read the mark, tell official from folk kilns, or date a piece? Look up standard reign-mark text, mark type, glaze color and common forgery tips by Ming/Qing era.
使用范围提示Scope notice 本工具只整理学界常见的官窑款识范式与书法特征，是鉴赏入门参考。同一朝代款识有早中晚变化、有官民窑差异，不能单凭底款断定真伪或年代。 This tool compiles commonly cited official-kiln mark conventions and calligraphic traits as a collecting primer. Marks vary within a reign and between official/folk kilns; the base mark alone cannot authenticate or date a piece.

① 选择朝代 / 帝王① Choose a reign

款识为该朝典型官窑范式；实物常有变体与民窑款。Marks shown are typical official-kiln conventions; objects often vary and include folk-kiln marks.

② 款识形态分类（图谱要点）② Mark-type categories

六字楷书款Six-char regular script

"大清××年制"六字两行或一行，端庄楷书。康熙、雍正朝典型，多为青花釉下。"Great Qing ×× era made" — six characters, upright regular script; typical Kangxi/Yongzheng, usually underglaze blue.

六字篆书款Six-char seal script

六字排成两行三列的篆书印章式，笔画匀净。乾隆朝盛行，笔力匀而板。Six characters in seal script arranged two rows of three; Qianlong-era hallmark, even and mechanical strokes.

四字 / 堂名款Four-char & hall marks

"××年制"四字或斋堂名（如"××堂制"），多为官窑定制或文人自用，款字较小精整。Four characters, or studio/hall names (e.g. "×× hall made"); refined custom pieces, neat small script.

花押款Shop / artist marks

纹样、符号、图画款，明晚至民窑常见，非纪年款，断代意义有限。Emblem/symbol/picture marks; common in late Ming and folk kilns, not dated, limited dating value.

寄托款Apocryphal marks

后朝写前朝年号（如清写"大明宣德年制"），非当时本朝，需结合胎釉青花判断。Later pieces bearing an earlier reign (e.g. Qing copying "Xuande"); date by body/glaze/blue, not the mark.

  1. 所列款识为学界常见范式与书法特征，同一朝代早中晚、官窑民窑都有变化，不是唯一标准。
  2. 底款可被后朝仿制、寄托或臆造，绝不能单凭款识判断真伪、断代或估价。
  3. 发色、釉上釉下为该朝典型倾向，非绝对；伪款多仿款字但难仿胎釉与老化痕迹。
  4. 本工具仅供鉴赏学习，不构成古董鉴定、断代、估价或投资建议。重要藏品请咨询合资格专家或独立第三方顾问。
  1. Marks shown are commonly cited conventions; they vary across early/mid/late reigns and between official and folk kilns — they are not the only standard.
  2. Base marks were routinely copied, apocryphally reused or invented in later periods; never rely on the mark alone to authenticate, date or value a piece.
  3. Glaze color and over/under-glaze are typical tendencies, not absolutes; fakes often copy the script but not the body, glaze or ageing.
