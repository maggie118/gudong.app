时间换算工具Time Converter
这个年份，年份对照多少年？ How many years ago?
输入公元或公元前年份，或选择明清年号，快速计算到当前公历年份的年数差。 Enter a CE or BCE year, or select a Ming or Qing reign title, to calculate the difference to the current Gregorian year.

① 输入年份① Enter a year

可输入 −3000 至 3000 年；负数表示公元前（如 −221 = 公元前 221 年），0 无效；选择年号后会自动填入其元年。Enter −3000 to 3000. Negative values mean BCE (e.g. −221 = 221 BCE); 0 is invalid. Selecting a reign title fills in its first year.
年号对照Reign era

② 选择明清年号② Select a Ming or Qing reign title

此处按年号元年计算，不代表某件器物的制作年份。This uses the first year of each reign and does not identify an object's date of manufacture.

免责声明Disclaimer 本工具仅按公历年份进行简单减法，公元前年份按天文纪年折算（不存在公元 0 年），结果用于时间参考，不考虑具体日期、历法转换或历史纪年争议。明清年号对应年份仅表示年号元年。改元之年先后行用的年号并列显示。年号数据取教育部《重編國語辭典修訂本》附录〈中國歷代年號表〉（521 条），以中国科学院自然科学史研究所〈中国历代年号索引表〉校核；两表存疑条照录。本工具不提供古董鉴定、断代、估价或投资建议；请结合可靠史料并咨询合资格专家。 This tool performs simple subtraction using Gregorian years. BCE years are converted using astronomical year numbering (no year 0). It does not account for exact dates, calendar conversion or historical dating disputes. A reign title's listed year is its first year only; when an era changed mid-year, both eras are shown. Reign-era data follows the Ministry of Education (Taiwan) dictionary appendix (521 eras), cross-checked against the CAS Institute for the History of Natural Sciences index. This tool does not provide antique authentication, dating, valuation or investment advice. Consult reliable sources and qualified specialists.
